# Broadcasting


## Vocabulary

- **Broadcasting**: Align trailing dimensions and expand axes of length one.

- **Compatible axes**: Equal lengths, or at least one length equal to one.


## Meaning

A matrix of shape (2,2) can add a column of shape (2,1). Each row offset repeats across that row. For matrix rows [1,2] and [1,2], offsets [10,20] yield [11,12] and [21,22]. Shapes (2,2) and (3,) are incompatible because trailing lengths two and three cannot match.


## Purpose

Broadcasting expresses useful repeated operations while shape mistakes can silently change meaning.


## Calculation

output[i,j] = matrix[i,j] + column[i,0]


### Worked case

Inputs: [1, 2, 10, 20]. Result: 22. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [3, 4, -1, 2]. Result: 6. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


## Explain

Can trailing axis lengths two and three broadcast?

No; neither is one and they differ

Compatible trailing axes must match or have length one.


## Debug

Adding a row vector when a column offset was intended.
