# Early stopping


## Vocabulary

- **Patience**: How long to wait without a sufficient validation improvement.

- **Best checkpoint**: Saved parameters from the best eligible validation result.


## Meaning

Track validation loss after training epochs. If it worsens while training loss keeps falling, stop according to a stated patience policy and restore the best checkpoint. This panel selects the lowest validation-loss epoch, using the earliest epoch for ties; it does not claim to simulate every patience rule. Test data must not choose the stopping point.


## Purpose

Stopping controls training duration without optimizing directly on the test set.


## Calculation

best epoch = argmin validation loss, with earliest tie
