# Training diagnostics


## Vocabulary

- **Diagnostic**: An observation that guides investigation.

- **Baseline**: A simple comparison model or rule.

- **Nonfinite value**: NaN or infinity, usually indicating a numerical failure.


## Meaning

Track training/validation losses, prediction distributions, per-class errors, gradient magnitudes and data alignment. A large loss gap is evidence to investigate, not a unique diagnosis. Compare with a simple baseline and inspect examples. If loss becomes nonfinite, stop and locate the first failing operation rather than displaying a success badge.


## Purpose

Diagnostics connect a failed outcome to a testable cause.


## Calculation

observed loss gap = validation loss - training loss

## Debug

Ignoring a NaN because the previous epoch looked good.

Check input types, labels, stable loss, learning rate and gradients in a deliberate order.
