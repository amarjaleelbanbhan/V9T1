# Checkpoints


## Vocabulary

- **Model state**: Parameters and persistent model buffers.

- **Optimizer state**: Moments, velocities and update count.

- **Resume metadata**: Epoch, scheduler, data order and random state as needed.


## Meaning

A checkpoint allows restoring an earlier state. For inference, model parameters and required buffers may be sufficient. Exact training continuation also needs optimizer state and relevant scheduler/random/data-loader state. Here a saved scalar weight is restored for a prediction; the changed current weight is deliberately ignored.


## Purpose

Saving only a screenshot or final loss cannot reproduce a model state.


## Calculation

restored prediction = saved weight × input


### Worked case

Inputs: [2, 9, 3]. Result: 6. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [-1, 9, 3]. Result: -3. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


## Explain

Is restoring weights alone always enough for exact training continuation?

No; optimizer and other relevant state may be required

Future updates can depend on state beyond parameters.


## Debug

Overwriting the only best checkpoint after every later epoch.

Compare inference restoration with an exact training-resume checkpoint.
