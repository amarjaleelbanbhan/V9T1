# Dropout


## Vocabulary

- **Drop rate**: Probability an activation is removed during training.

- **Inverted scaling**: Divide retained values by the keep probability.


## Meaning

With activation 2 and drop rate 0.5, a kept value becomes 4 and a removed value becomes 0. Their expectation is 2 under the specified Bernoulli mask. At inference, return the unchanged activation. This visualization uses a fixed mask so the arithmetic can be predicted; actual training typically samples masks.


## Purpose

Dropout changes training behavior and can reduce reliance on particular activations.


## Calculation

training output = activation × mask/(1-p)


### Worked case

Inputs: [2, 0.5, 1]. Result: 4. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [2, 0.5, 0]. Result: 0. Intermediate operations are available in the app.
