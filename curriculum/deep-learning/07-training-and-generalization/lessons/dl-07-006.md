# Initialization in training


## Vocabulary

- **Signal scale**: Magnitude propagated across layers.

- **Gradient scale**: Magnitude of loss sensitivity.


## Meaning

Initializer assumptions interact with activation functions and fan-in. He variance 2/fan-in is commonly paired with ReLU. This scale alone does not guarantee training: data scaling, optimization, depth and normalization also matter. Record initialization and random seeds to reproduce an experiment.


## Purpose

An unstable initial signal can make training fail before meaningful fitting begins.


## Calculation

He standard deviation = sqrt(2/fan-in)
