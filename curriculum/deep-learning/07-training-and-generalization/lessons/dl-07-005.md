# Batch normalization


## Vocabulary

- **Batch mean**: Mean of activations for one feature in the batch.

- **Batch variance**: Spread around that mean.

- **Gamma and beta**: Learned scaling and shifting.


## Meaning

For one feature with batch values 1 and 3, mean is 2 and biased variance is 1. Normalize the first value to -1 when epsilon is zero; gamma two gives -2 with beta zero. A small positive epsilon prevents division by zero. Training uses batch statistics; ordinary evaluation uses running statistics. Framework running-variance conventions need separate care.


## Purpose

Normalization behavior depends on batch statistics and training mode.


## Calculation

output = gamma × (x - mean)/sqrt(variance + epsilon) + beta
