# Regularization and weight decay


## Vocabulary

- **Regularization**: A constraint or penalty discouraging undesirable fitting behavior.

- **L2 penalty**: An objective term proportional to squared weights.


## Meaning

Adding λw²/2 gives penalty gradient λw. An SGD update becomes w_new = w - η(g_data + λw). With no data gradient, w shrinks toward zero. Coupled L2 gradient and decoupled weight decay can differ under adaptive optimizers; do not treat every framework option as identical.


## Purpose

Regularization trades training fit against a preference for simpler parameter values.


## Calculation

w_new = w - η(g_data + λw)
