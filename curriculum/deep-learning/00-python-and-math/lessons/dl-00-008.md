# Derivatives and chain rule


## Vocabulary

- **Derivative**: Local rate of change of an output with an input.

- **Intermediate**: A value between input and final output.

- **Chain rule**: Multiply derivatives along a composition.


## Meaning

Let u = s × x and y = u². First find dy/du = 2u, then du/dx = s. Multiply to get dy/dx = 2s²x. At x = 2 and s = 3, u = 6 and the derivative is 36. The derivative is a sensitivity, not the output y. A small input change Δx gives approximately derivative × Δx change in y.


## Purpose

Backpropagation applies this rule across many connected operations.


## Calculation

dy/dx = (dy/du)(du/dx) = 2s²x


### Worked case

Inputs: [2, 3]. Result: 36. The app exposes intermediate operations step by step.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [-1, 2]. Result: -8. The app exposes intermediate operations step by step.


## Explain

How do local derivatives combine along this path?

Multiply them

A small x change first changes u; that u change then changes y. The two scale factors multiply.
