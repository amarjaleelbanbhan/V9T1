# Probability, mean and variance


## Vocabulary

- **Mean**: Sum divided by count.

- **Population variance**: Mean squared distance from the mean.

- **Probability**: A number from zero to one describing uncertainty.


## Meaning

For equally likely outcomes, probability is favorable outcomes divided by all outcomes. The mean describes a center. Variance measures spread using squared deviations, so negative and positive deviations do not cancel. This lesson computes population variance, dividing by n. Estimating variance from a sample commonly uses n - 1 instead.


## Purpose

Models reason about uncertainty and normalize data using statistics.


## Calculation

μ = Σx/n; variance = Σ(x - μ)²/n

## Explain

Why square deviations before averaging?

So distances do not cancel and larger deviations weigh more

Raw deviations from the mean sum to zero; squaring measures their magnitude.


## Debug

Dividing by n - 1 while labeling the result population variance.

For [1,2,3], mean = 2, squared deviations = [1,0,1], variance = 2/3. For a fair die, P(even) = 3/6.
