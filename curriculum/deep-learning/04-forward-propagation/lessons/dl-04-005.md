# Logits versus probabilities


## Vocabulary

- **Logit**: An unrestricted raw score.

- **Probability**: A normalized value interpreted under a model.


## Meaning

Raw logits can be negative, positive or much larger than one. Softmax converts a set of logits to nonnegative numbers summing to one. Larger logits increase relative probability, but probabilities still require calibration for reliable confidence claims. A predicted class usually selects the largest score or probability.


## Purpose

Correct interpretation prevents treating raw scores as confidence percentages.


## Calculation

p₁ = exp(z₁ - max)/(exp(z₁ - max)+exp(z₂ - max))
