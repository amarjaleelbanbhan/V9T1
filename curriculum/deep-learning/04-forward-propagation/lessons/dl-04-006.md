# Training versus inference


## Vocabulary

- **Training mode**: Behavior used while learning parameters.

- **Inference mode**: Behavior used for prediction after training.

- **Dropout mask**: Values deciding which activations are retained.


## Meaning

Training can use randomness such as dropout and batch statistics. Inference generally disables dropout and uses learned normalization statistics. In inverted dropout, a kept activation is divided by its keep probability during training; inference returns the activation unchanged. A fixed mask is shown here for reproducibility, not sampled randomly.


## Purpose

Forgetting the mode can produce inconsistent predictions.


## Calculation

training output = x × mask/(1 - drop probability)
