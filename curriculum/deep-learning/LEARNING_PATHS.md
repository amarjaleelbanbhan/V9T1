# Learning paths

- Absolute beginner: 00 → 01 → 02 → 03 → 04 → 05 → 06 → 07. Alternate visual predictions with small actual Python calculations.
- Practitioner: finish the core, then 08 → 09 for vision or 10 → 11 → 12 for text; train and evaluate one reproducible framework model.
- Advanced: 13 and 14 after their prerequisites, then 15 → 16 → 17. Benchmark actual resource tradeoffs and failure cases.
- Research/engineering: 18 after deployment and evaluation competence; reproduce a bounded paper claim and document negative results.
- Exam revision: existing nine labs remain directly available. Add diagnostic questions and delayed mixed review without requiring the whole advanced track.

Revision proposal: revisit mistakes after 1, 3 and 7 days, with changed numbers and explanations. This schedule and the mastery threshold need learner testing; they are design hypotheses. Progress must distinguish visited, attempted, passed and retained evidence. No progress bar alone grants mastery.
