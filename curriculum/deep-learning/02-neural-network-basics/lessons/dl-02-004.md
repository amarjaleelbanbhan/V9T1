# Linear layers


## Vocabulary

- **Affine layer**: A linear transformation plus a bias.

- **Output unit**: One dot product with a column of weights, plus its bias.


## Meaning

A dense layer applies the same affine operation to every example. Multiple output units use different weight columns. Our panel computes one output from two inputs; the forward lab extends this to more layers. Two affine layers without a nonlinear activation combine into one affine transformation.


## Purpose

The layer equation is the foundation of neural computation.

## Explain

Why insert nonlinear activations between dense layers?

To represent functions beyond one affine transformation

Composing affine transformations remains affine; nonlinearities expand expressive power.


## Debug

Calling every affine output a probability.

A raw affine output can be negative or exceed one.
