# Sigmoid, tanh, ReLU and leaky ReLU


## Vocabulary

- **Activation**: A function applied to a preactivation.

- **Saturation**: A region with very small local sensitivity.

- **Leaky slope**: The multiplier on negative inputs.


## Meaning

Sigmoid maps to (0,1); tanh maps to (-1,1). ReLU keeps positive inputs and maps negatives to zero. Leaky ReLU retains a scaled negative branch. The panel displays all four results and asks for leaky ReLU. Sigmoid and tanh saturate at large magnitudes. ReLU has a corner at zero, where a chosen subgradient convention is needed.


## Purpose

Nonlinear activations let stacked layers represent more than affine functions.


## Calculation

leaky ReLU(z) = z if z ≥ 0, otherwise αz
