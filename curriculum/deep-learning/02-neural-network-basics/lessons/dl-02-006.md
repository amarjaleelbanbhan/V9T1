# Stable softmax


## Vocabulary

- **Logit**: A raw score before probability normalization.

- **Softmax**: Exponentials normalized to sum to one.

- **Stabilization**: Subtract a shared maximum before exponentiation.


## Meaning

Exponentiating logits like 1000 directly can overflow. Subtracting their maximum preserves softmax probabilities because the common scale cancels. For logits [1000,1001], use [-1,0]. The first probability is exp(-1)/(exp(-1)+1). Each probability depends on all logits, unlike separate scalar sigmoid outputs.


## Purpose

Stable normalization prevents avoidable numerical failures.


## Calculation

pᵢ = exp(zᵢ - max(z)) / Σexp(zⱼ - max(z))


### Worked case

Inputs: [1000, 1001]. Result: 0.2689414213699951. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [0, 0]. Result: 0.5. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


## Explain

Why can the maximum be subtracted from every logit?

The common exponential scale cancels in normalization

Both numerator and denominator share the same factor; their ratio is unchanged.
