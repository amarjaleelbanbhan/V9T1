# Chain rule


## Vocabulary

- **Composition**: One operation takes the result of another.

- **Path derivative**: Product of sensitivities along one path.


## Meaning

For u = sx and y = u², a small change in x first changes u by s times that change. It then changes y by 2u times the u change. Multiply these factors. This gives 2s²x, with sign determined by x when s² is positive.


## Purpose

The chain rule transmits loss sensitivity back through layers.


## Calculation

dy/dx = (2u) × s = 2s²x


### Worked case

Inputs: [1, 2]. Result: 8. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [-2, 2]. Result: -16. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


## Explain

Along one composed path, do sensitivities add or multiply?

Multiply

One operation scales the change produced by the preceding operation.


## Debug

Adding local derivatives along a path.

If local factors are three and four, the path sensitivity is twelve, not seven.
