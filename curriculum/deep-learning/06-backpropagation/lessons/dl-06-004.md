# Parameter gradients


## Vocabulary

- **Weight gradient**: Sensitivity to a weight; delta times its input in a dense unit.

- **Bias gradient**: The delta, since the bias enters additively.


## Meaning

Once a hidden delta is known, multiply it by the input feeding that weight. In the worked case hidden delta is 9 and x is 2, so the first weight gradient is 18. The hidden bias gradient is 9. Batch gradients combine per-example contributions using the chosen sum or mean convention.


## Purpose

Gradients identify which parameters should change and by how much.


## Calculation

dL/dw = delta × input; dL/db = delta


### Worked case

Inputs: [2, 1, 0, 3, 1, 4]. Result: 18. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [1, 1, 0, 3, 1, 2]. Result: 6. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


## Explain

Why does the bias gradient equal delta here?

The derivative of z with respect to its additive bias is one

The chain rule multiplies delta by dz/db = 1.
