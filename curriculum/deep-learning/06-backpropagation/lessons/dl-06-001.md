# Local derivatives


## Vocabulary

- **Local derivative**: Sensitivity of one operation to its immediate input.

- **Upstream derivative**: Sensitivity arriving from later operations.


## Meaning

The operation y = x² has local derivative 2x. At x = 2 it is 4; at x = -2 it is -4. A derivative is not necessarily positive and is not the function value. Backpropagation multiplies this local derivative by the derivative arriving from later loss operations.


## Purpose

Small operation rules assemble into gradients for a whole network.


## Calculation

d(x²)/dx = 2x


### Worked case

Inputs: [2]. Result: 4. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [-2]. Result: -4. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


## Explain

Can a derivative be negative?

Yes; increasing the input can decrease the output locally

At a negative x, moving toward zero lowers x².


## Debug

Using the operation output as its derivative.

At x = 3, x² is 9 but its derivative is 6.
