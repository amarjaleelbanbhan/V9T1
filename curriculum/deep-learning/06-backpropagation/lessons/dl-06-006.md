# Finite differences


## Vocabulary

- **Perturbation**: A small input change used to estimate sensitivity.

- **Central difference**: Uses a positive and negative perturbation.


## Meaning

Estimate the derivative of f(x) = x² with (f(x+h)-f(x-h))/(2h). At x = 2 and h = 0.001, the result is about 4. Large h can introduce truncation error for general functions; very small h can cause floating-point cancellation. Avoid nondifferentiable corners when comparing smooth derivatives.


## Purpose

Gradient checks catch algebraic or implementation errors.


## Calculation

central difference = [f(x+h)-f(x-h)]/(2h)


### Worked case

Inputs: [2, 0.001]. Result: 4. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [-1, 0.001]. Result: -2. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


## Explain

Is the smallest possible h always the most accurate?

No; rounding and cancellation can dominate

Finite precision creates a tradeoff between truncation and rounding error.
