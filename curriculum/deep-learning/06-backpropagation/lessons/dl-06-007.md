# Vanishing and exploding gradients


## Vocabulary

- **Vanishing**: Repeated factors make sensitivity approach zero.

- **Exploding**: Repeated factors increase sensitivity greatly.


## Meaning

Backpropagation multiplies local factors. Four factors of 0.5 produce 0.0625, while four factors of 2 produce 16. Actual networks have matrix Jacobians and multiple routes, so this scalar path is an illustration. Activation saturation, initialization, normalization and architecture affect gradient behavior; clipping limits magnitude but does not restore a vanished signal.


## Purpose

Poor sensitivity can prevent earlier layers from learning usefully.


## Calculation

path gradient = product of local factors

Inputs: [0.5, 4]. Result: 0.0625. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

## Explain

Does clipping an exploding gradient recreate a vanished gradient?

No; these are different problems

Clipping caps a large magnitude; it does not amplify a near-zero gradient path.


## Debug

Assuming depth alone guarantees either failure mode.

A chain of factors equal to one preserves its sensitivity at any tested depth.
