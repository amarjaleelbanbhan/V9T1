# SGD, momentum and Adam


## Vocabulary

- **SGD**: An update using a sample or mini-batch gradient.

- **Momentum**: Retains a velocity across updates.

- **Adam**: Uses first and second gradient moments with bias correction.


## Meaning

Plain SGD uses w - ηg. This momentum convention uses v_new = βv_old + g, then w - ηv_new. Adam maintains m and v as exponential averages, corrects their early-step bias, then scales m_hat by sqrt(v_hat)+epsilon. The panel compares SGD, momentum and a freshly initialized first Adam step; it does not reuse the momentum velocity as Adam state.


## Purpose

Optimizer state changes how a sequence of gradients affects weights.


## Calculation

momentum: v = βv_old + g; w_new = w - ηv
