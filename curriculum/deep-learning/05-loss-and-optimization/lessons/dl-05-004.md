# Gradient descent


## Vocabulary

- **Gradient**: Sensitivity of loss to a parameter.

- **Learning rate**: The multiplier controlling update size.


## Meaning

For L(w) = w², the derivative is 2w. The update w_new = w - η(2w) moves opposite the derivative. Starting at 2 with rate 0.1 gives 1.6, lowering loss from 4 to 2.56. Starting at -2 gives -1.6. A large step can overshoot; a small loss on one step does not prove convergence.


## Purpose

Training changes parameters to improve the chosen objective.


## Calculation

w_new = w - η × dL/dw


### Worked case

Inputs: [2, 0.1]. Result: 1.6. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [-2, 0.1]. Result: -1.6. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


## Explain

Why subtract the derivative in this update?

To move in the local direction of decreasing loss

A negative derivative causes the weight to increase; the direction follows local loss sensitivity.


## Debug

Adding the gradient and thereby ascending the loss.

At w = -2 the update increases w toward zero, while decreasing the loss.
