# MSE and MAE


## Vocabulary

- **Residual**: Prediction minus target.

- **MAE**: Mean absolute residual.

- **MSE**: Mean squared residual.


## Meaning

For errors [1,-2], absolute errors are [1,2], giving MAE 1.5. Squared errors are [1,4], giving MSE 2.5. Squaring emphasizes large errors and changes units. State whether your loss uses a sum, a mean, or a half factor; its derivative depends on that convention.


## Purpose

The objective defines what training tries to improve.


## Calculation

MSE = mean(error²); MAE = mean(abs(error))


### Worked case

Inputs: [1, -2]. Result: 2.5. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [0, 2]. Result: 2. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


## Explain

Which objective weighs a large residual more strongly?

MSE through squaring

Absolute value removes sign but grows linearly; squared error grows quadratically.


## Debug

Opposite errors can cancel in a signed mean, despite both being wrong.
