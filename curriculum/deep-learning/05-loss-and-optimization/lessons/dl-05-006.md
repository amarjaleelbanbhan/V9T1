# Learning-rate schedules


## Vocabulary

- **Schedule**: A rule changing learning rate during training.

- **Epoch**: One traversal of the training set.


## Meaning

An exponential schedule sets η_k = η_0 × decay^k. Starting at 0.1 with decay 0.5 gives 0.025 at epoch two. Scheduling can make later updates smaller, but an inappropriate initial rate or fast decay can prevent learning. Keep scheduler timing consistent: stepping per batch differs from stepping per epoch.


## Purpose

Step size often needs to change as training progresses.


## Calculation

η_k = η_0 × decay^k


### Worked case

Inputs: [0.1, 0.5, 2]. Result: 0.025. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

## Explain

Does stepping the same schedule every batch match stepping every epoch?

No; the number of schedule updates differs

An epoch can contain many updates; timing changes the effective decay.


## Debug

Decaying too many times because scheduler timing is unclear.

If there are ten batches per epoch, batch-based decay applies ten times as often.
