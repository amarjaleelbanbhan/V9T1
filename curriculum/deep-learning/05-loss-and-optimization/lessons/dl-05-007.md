# Epochs and mini-batches


## Vocabulary

- **Mini-batch**: A subset used for one optimizer update.

- **Update**: One parameter adjustment after a gradient calculation.


## Meaning

Five examples with batch size two give batches of sizes two, two and one if the final partial batch is retained. Two epochs produce six updates. Dropping the final partial batch would change the count. Loss averaging over a smaller final batch still needs a deliberate convention.


## Purpose

Batch and epoch counts determine update frequency and reproducibility.


## Calculation

updates = ceil(N / batch size) × epochs


### Worked case

Inputs: [5, 2, 2]. Result: 6. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


### Transfer case

Inputs: [5, 3, 2]. Result: 4. Intermediate operations are available in the app.


## Explain

Is one epoch always one optimizer update?

No; it can contain several mini-batch updates

Each mini-batch can produce a separate update.
