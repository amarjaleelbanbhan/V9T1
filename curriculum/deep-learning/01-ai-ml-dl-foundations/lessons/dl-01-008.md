# Bias, variance and generalization


## Vocabulary

- **Underfitting**: The model fails to capture useful structure.

- **Overfitting**: The model captures training-specific patterns poorly transferable to new data.

- **Generalization**: Performance on relevant unseen data.


## Meaning

A high training loss and high validation loss can indicate underfitting. Very low training loss with higher validation loss can indicate overfitting, data shift or other defects. The gap is validation loss minus training loss in this toy view. It is a diagnostic clue, not a proof. Compare datasets, metrics and uncertainty before choosing a fix.


## Purpose

You need useful future predictions, not only a small training loss.


## Calculation

gap = validation loss - training loss
