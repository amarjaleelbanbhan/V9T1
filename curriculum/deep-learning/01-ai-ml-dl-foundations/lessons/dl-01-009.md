# Neural-network history


## Vocabulary

- **Perceptron**: An early trainable linear threshold model.

- **Backpropagation**: Efficient reverse application of local derivatives.

- **AlexNet**: A 2012 convolutional vision milestone.


## Meaning

Rosenblatt described the perceptron in 1958. Rumelhart, Hinton and Williams popularized training multilayer networks by backpropagation in 1986; related reverse differentiation work predates that paper. AlexNet showed major image-recognition gains in 2012. Data, compute, algorithms and evaluation all contributed; progress is not a story of one invention replacing everything.
